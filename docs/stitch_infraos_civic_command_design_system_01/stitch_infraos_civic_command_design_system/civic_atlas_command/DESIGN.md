## Brand & Style

The visual identity embodies directly institutional and accessible civic utility. It pairs a pragmatic neo-grotesque typographical backbone with balanced structural cards and deep graphite command architecture. Designed for national infrastructure intelligence, territorial surveying, and inter-agency coordination, the system communicates sovereign authority, cartographic exactitude, and administrative clarity.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Visual Style:** Institutional Civic Utility and Architectural Precision. The design merges archival civil engineering draftsmanship, surveyor chainage documentation, and high-readiness operations command.
- **Atmosphere:** Rigorous, unhurried, calm, and mathematically disciplined. It replaces ephemeral consumer software trends with stable structural grids, crisp hairline boundaries, and high-legibility telemetry.
- **Dual-Surface Philosophy:** Clear functional partition between master command orchestration and operational execution. Command surfaces utilize deep graphite enclosures to focus decision-making, while execution workspaces leverage crisp, light neutral planes optimized for long-duration documentation review and spatial analysis.

## Colors

The color system enforces a dual-surface operational hierarchy. Colors are functional instruments for systemic status, spatial conflicts, and chainage telemetry, rather than decorative embellishments.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#14202D`):** Deep Graphite. The sovereign anchor applied to master command navigation rails, command headers, and core framework surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#FF5B00`):** Surgical Orange. Used selectively for focused survey points, active indicators, and real-time operational states.
- **Tertiary (`#A83900`):** High-Contrast Accessible Orange. Calibrated to guarantee WCAG 2.1 AA compliance (5.7:1+ contrast) against light backgrounds for critical text buttons, links, and high-visibility survey station badges.
- **Neutral (`#111827`):** Deep Sovereign Ink. The primary typography color, calibrated to reduce eye strain across dense tabular data grids.

### Surface Tiers & Support Tones
- **Operational Canvas (`#F7F9FB`):** Clear neutral plane for core daylight workspaces and map interfaces.
- **Drafting Canvas (`#FAF8F4`):** Warm drafting tone reserved for alignment profiles, survey sheets, and cross-section schematics.
- **Pure Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-density cards, metric modules, data tables, and input containers.
- **Structural Outlines:** `#E2E8F0` for default structural card divisions, `#CBD5E1` for input field boundaries and table header rules, and `#273449` for dark command boundaries.

### Operational Status Palette
- **Critical Clashes & Failures (`#8C1F28` / `#93000A`):** Spatial overlaps, trenching conflicts, and overdue permits. Paired with container `#FDECEE`.
- **Review & Pending Approvals (`#FEB600` / `#6C5311`):** Utility relocation advisories and provisional estimates. Paired with container `#FFF8E6`.
- **Cleared & Verified Clearances (`#279541` / `#166B30`):** Permitted works and resolved conflicts. Paired with container `#ECFDF3`.

## Typography

Public Sans serves as the sole typographic engine, providing crisp, institutional neo-grotesque proportions across all display tiers, narrative structures, and control interfaces.

### Typographic Principles
- **Clarity Over Embellishment:** Public Sans was developed for official civic clarity. Its geometric discipline, tall x-height, and open counters guarantee legibility across complex multi-column telemetry, chainage stations, and administrative dossiers.
- **Tabular Alignment:** All numerical indicators, metric readouts, currency values, and station markers use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to maintain strict vertical scanning alignment.
- **Multi-Script Integration:** When rendering Sinhala or Tamil copy (`:lang(si)`, `:lang(ta)`), line-height expands dynamically to 1.75 to accommodate complex glyph configurations and stacked vowel diacritics without vertical clipping. Uppercase transformations and letter-spacing modifications are strictly disabled in Indic contexts.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by an architectural 12-column responsive fluid grid structured around a 4px base increment and 24px major divisions, reflecting the discipline of civil engineering drafting sheets.

### Layout Philosophy
- **Command Shell Architecture:** A fixed 256px (`16rem`) Command Navigation rail anchors the interface on desktop screens, locking primary operational controls while allowing the secondary operational workspace to fluidly respond to screen widths up to 1440px (`90rem`). Live geospatial mapping interfaces (GIS canvases) expand to fill the remaining screen space.
- **Drafting Grid Alignment:** Content rows adhere to a dual-axis structural grid. Card dimensions and control ribbons snap to predictable vertical and horizontal coordinate intervals.

### Form Factor Behavior
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** Fixed Deep Graphite command rail, 12-column data grid with 32px gutters, and synchronized multi-panel inspector drawers.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** Command rail collapses into a high-contrast slide-over drawer; 8-column layout with 24px gutters; multi-column survey matrices unlock horizontal scrolling with sticky identity columns.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** Single-column vertical stacking with 16px margins; chainage telemetry ribbons convert into sequential card blocks; touch targets observe a mandatory minimum footprint of 44px × 44px.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through architectural surface layering and crisp structural outlines rather than heavy atmospheric dropshadows. Depth reflects physical drafting sheets placed neatly atop an operations console.

### Structural Layering & Rules
- **Outlines Over Shadows:** Components separate through hairline borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0` on light canvases; `1px solid #273449` on command panels). High-density interfaces maintain clean, uncluttered visual planes.
- **Tonal Stepping:** Surfaces establish visual priority through tone:
  - Base background: `#F7F9FB`
  - Active review containers: `#FFFFFF`
  - Technical drafting sheets: `#FAF8F4`
  - Command command chrome: `#14202D`
  - Elevated command flyouts: `#17212D`
- **Ambient Shadow Lift:** Reserved exclusively for floating interaction drawers, active dropdown menus, and dragging nodes:
  - Default: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`
  - Overlay Dialogs: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(20, 32, 45, 0.12), 0 2px 6px rgba(20, 32, 45, 0.04)`

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft structural radius (`roundedness: 1`), instilling an architectural, technical-instrument finish that rejects soft, playful consumer treatments.

### Corner Radii Conventions
- **Base Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges, Tabs):** `0.25rem` (`4px`). Delivers a precise, calibrated edge resembling technical drafting rulers and field instruments.
- **Containers & Tiles (Data Cards, Modal Panels, Flyouts):** `0.5rem` (`8px`, `rounded-lg`). Creates structured boundary separation while keeping content lines compact and rectilinear.
- **Large Context Shells (Map Viewports, Sheet Enclosures):** `0.75rem` (`12px`, `rounded-xl`).
- **Telemetry Chips & Pill Marks:** `9999px` (`rounded-full`) used strictly for survey status nodes and chainage point markers.

## Components

Component structures emphasize legibility, predictable ergonomics, and multi-channel status clarity across civil infrastructure applications.

### Buttons
- **Primary:** High-Contrast Deep Orange (`#A83900`) background with crisp white typography. Height: 40px (`2.5rem`), padding: `0.5rem 1rem`, corner radius: `0.25rem` (`4px`). Minimum touch boundary is strictly 44px on mobile devices.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#111827`. Hover shifts background to `#F1F5F9`.
- **Command Action (On Dark):** Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, border `1px solid #273449`, text `#F8FAFC`.
- **Destructive:** Background `#FDECEE`, border `1px solid #8C1F28`, text `#8C1F28`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Fields:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface framed by `1px solid #CBD5E1` border, corner radius `0.25rem`. Height: 40px.
- **Focus Rings:** Non-negotiable high-visibility `2px solid #FF5B00` outline with an offset of 2px.
- **Survey & Coordinate Inputs:** Formatted with tabular numbers and monospace tracking for road chainage (e.g., `0+000`, `12+450`) and GIS coordinates.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Structural 16px frames with a `1.5px` border. Checked states feature solid fills in `#A83900` or `#14202D` with white iconography.

### Cards & Operational Tiles
- **Structure:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px solid #E2E8F0` hairline border and `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`) radius.
- **Top Indicator Rail:** A 3px top accent bar applied to operational data cards:
  - Active Works: `#FF5B00`
  - Critical Spatial Clashes: `#8C1F28`
  - Awaiting Review / Inter-Agency Approvals: `#FEB600`
  - Cleared / Completed Milestones: `#279541`

### The Infrastructure Thread & Route Linework
- A signature 1.5px route linework motif visually joins navigation nodes, excavation permits, and corridor milestones.
- **Status Node Shapes (Dual-Channel Accessibility):**
  - Planned / Draft: Open Ring (`○`)
  - Active: Solid Circle (`●`)
  - Delay / Spatial Clash: Solid Diamond (`◆`)
  - Verified / Approved: Checkmark Node (`✓`)
  - Rejected: Enclosed Cross (`⊗`)

### Honesty Chips
- Non-decorative, dashed-outline status badges informing operators of data veracity:
  - `[ PROVISIONAL ]`, `[ REPORTED ]`, `[ UNAVAILABLE ]`, `[ CERTIFIED ]`
  - Rendered with transparent backgrounds, 1px dashed `#CBD5E1` border, and compact, uppercase Public Sans typography.
